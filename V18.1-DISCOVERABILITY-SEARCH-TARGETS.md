# V18.1 — Discoverability Search Targets

JYC's SEO strategy is built around real JIIT/JYC intents rather than keyword stuffing. These are target queries where the website can build topical relevance; rankings are not guaranteed and depend on indexing, authority, competition, freshness and external signals.

## Product changes made in V18.1
- Homepage metadata and visible copy now naturally connect JIIT Youth Club with JIIT clubs, student communities, events and fests.
- Clubs page explicitly explains that it is the official JIIT club list and covers student societies.
- Events page explicitly describes JIIT events, campus events, workshops and competitions.
- Fests page explicitly describes JIIT fests and flagship JYC events.
- About page answers the useful intent “What is JIIT Youth Club?” directly.
- Calendar title/description targets the JIIT event-calendar intent.
- Recruitment metadata targets club recruitment/audition intent.
- `llms.txt` includes the broader search vocabulary for discovery systems.
- Organization structured data recognizes `JYC` and `JIIT JYC` as alternate names.
- Public club/event internal links use readable slugs, improving crawlable topical URLs.
- The sitemap generator includes public club/event URLs when production environment variables are configured.

## Important content rule
Only use specific club names, fest names, dates, venues and event claims when they are officially published by JYC or supported by the site's approved data. Do not create fake pages just to capture searches.

## Research basis
Current public JIIT material describes JYC as the central/apex student body coordinating clubs, events and fests, and current JIIT material references Impressions and Converge as major JYC-connected fests. This supports the topical structure above.
