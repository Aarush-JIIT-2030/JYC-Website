# JYC Website V18.9.6 — Club Redesign

## Product direction
JYC is presented as a **club website first**, not as a general student utility portal.

Primary public hierarchy:

**JYC → Clubs → Events → Fests → Team → Gallery → Recruitment → My JYC**

Supporting utilities remain contextual rather than competing with JYC.

## Public cleanup
- Removed Discover from public navigation; legacy `/discover` redirects to Clubs.
- Removed the Student Guide, Resources, Notifications and academic/campus utility promotion from the public shell.
- Legacy `/guide`, `/resources` and `/notifications` routes redirect to JYC-first destinations for compatibility.
- Campus Map remains available contextually for event venues, but is no longer promoted as a core public feature.
- JIIT Pulse promotion was removed from the public campus map.
- More menu now contains JYC story, Clubs, Events, Fests, Team, Gallery, Recruitment, Calendar, My JYC, Contact, JYC Assistant and staff access.

## Homepage redesign
- New clubhouse-style hero centered on JYC identity and Phoenix artwork.
- Three Phoenix functions: Communities, Experiences, Your JYC.
- JYC story is a clickable section.
- Club communities, upcoming experience, recruitment, flagship experiences, team, gallery and archive are the main content rhythm.
- External/student utilities no longer dominate the bottom of the homepage.
- Footer is JYC-specific rather than platform/product marketing.

## Interaction
- Tutorial is viewport-fixed and navigates through Clubs → Events → Fests → My JYC → More.
- Custom cursor, Phoenix motion, atmospheric stars and light/dark treatment are preserved.
- CSS-native animation only; no new runtime dependency was introduced.

## Admin
Existing Control Center, draft-first AI workflow, system health and readable admin navigation remain intact.

## Open-source design references
The redesign borrows hierarchy/composition ideas from public student-activity portals such as IIT Bombay Students' Gymkhana and the open-code/composable UI philosophy of shadcn/ui. No shadcn package was added to the runtime; the JYC design system remains custom.
